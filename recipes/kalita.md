# Kalita Wave notes

## Baseline recipe

20g coffee to 310g water (about 1:15.5), water at 92-95°C. The flat bed and three
small holes make the Wave more forgiving than the V60 and slower-draining by design.
Bloom 30-45 seconds, then pour in 3-4 small pulses, keeping the water level low.
Target drawdown 3:00 to 4:30.

## Grind

Medium — slightly coarser than a V60 grind for the same coffee, because the flat-bottom
brewer restricts flow on its own. The grind-first rule still applies: it is the
highest-leverage variable, but back off if the drawdown is already slow.

## Temperature

Same guidance as any pour-over: 94-96°C for light roasts, 88-92°C for dark. Raise
temperature instead of grinding finer when the cup is sour but drainage is already
borderline.

## Drawdown

The Wave stalls more readily than the V60 when the grind produces many fines, because
the paper seats flush against the flat bed. If drawdown runs past 5:00, grind coarser
and pour more gently; do not stir late in the brew.

## Common defects

Same defect logic as the V60: sour/thin = underextracted (finer or hotter), bitter
and drying = overextracted (coarser or cooler), weak but balanced = ratio problem
(more coffee), hollow = uneven bed (pour technique). The Wave's most common failure
is the slow stall from over-agitation — pour gently onto the bed, not into it.
