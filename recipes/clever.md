# Clever Dripper notes

## Baseline recipe

18g coffee to 280g water (about 1:15.5), water at 92-95°C. The Clever is an immersion
brewer with a valve: steep 2-3 minutes with the valve closed, then set it on the
server to release. Because extraction happens mostly during the steep, drawdown time
matters less than on percolation brewers — target 1:00 to 2:00 of drainage after
release.

## Grind

Medium-coarse. Immersion extracts more evenly and less aggressively than percolation,
so the Clever tolerates a wider grind range. Finer grinds raise extraction and slow
the release drainage; adjust one step at a time.

## Steep time

Steep time substitutes for some of what grind does on a pour-over: a longer steep
raises extraction without adding fines. If the cup is sour and the drainage is
already slow, extend the steep 30 seconds before touching the grind.

## Drawdown

Post-release drainage past 2:00 means the fines are sealing the filter, usually from
grinding too fine or agitating hard before release. Grind coarser or skip the
pre-release stir.

## Common defects

Sour/thin: grind finer, steep longer, or raise temperature. Bitter/astringent:
coarser, cooler, or shorter steep. Weak but balanced: more coffee (immersion brews
read weaker than pour-overs at the same ratio, so 1:15 is a reasonable default).
